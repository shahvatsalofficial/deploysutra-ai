Self-audit highlights

The audit plants canary strings in env values, Secret data, annotations, plan variables, prior state and addresses, then searches every output: none may appear. It feeds 15 hostile YAML inputs and 400 seeded mutations to the parser, checks determinism and tamper detection, and verifies that no corpus resource can produce any gate except BLOCKED or HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED even with all rollback boxes ticked.
